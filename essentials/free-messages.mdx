## Share your experience with AppJet and get 25 free messages!

No matter your current plan (free or paid) you can earn 25 credits by sharing on X or Linkedin your experience with AppJet:

  - Tag @appjetai on X or LinkedIn,
  - Write a 200 characters minimum text about your experience with AppJet,
  - Your account must have at least 100 followers and be active (excluding fake/empty followers),
  - Add a screenshot or a video of your project made with AppJet (Optional),
  - Add a link to your project (Optional)
  - Open a ticket with our Help Support System with the link to your post, you can also email us at support@appjet.ai

  <tip>Accounts with strong activity, posts with several replies and shares are processed in priority.</tip>

## Report a bug or suggest a new feature to get 25 more free messages!

Another way to get 25 more messages is to report a bug or suggest a new feature that we select to be implemented in the next versions of AppJet!

Send us an email or open a ticket from AppJet (Help section) to submit your idea or discovery. Don't forgot to mention your Github username!
